Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to PomTom, a Pomodoro-based focus timer app built to help you stay productive and reach your goals.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user. By using PomTom, you agree to the practices described here.

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Information We Collect

PomTom is designed to work primarily offline. Most data (sessions, goals, stats, settings) is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

The following data may be collected automatically:

  • Advertising data – PomTom displays ads powered by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob). These networks may collect device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID), IP address, and usage data to serve relevant ads. Please refer to the respective ad network's privacy policy for details.
  • Crash & diagnostics data – If the app crashes, anonymous crash logs may be collected to help us improve stability.

We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information directly.

How We Use Your Information

  • To display relevant advertisements and support the free version of the app.
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues.
  • To improve app performance and user experience.

Third-Party Services

PomTom uses the following third-party services that may collect data independently:

  • Google AdMob – for advertising
  • Google Firebase – for crash reporting (if applicable)

We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

Data Retention & Storage

All timer sessions, goals, and productivity stats are stored exclusively on your device. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete this data.

We do not maintain any server-side database of your personal activity.

Children's Privacy

PomTom is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

  • Opt out of personalized advertising (via your device's Advertising ID settings).
  • Request deletion of any data associated with you.
  • Access information about data collected through third-party ad networks.

To opt out of personalized ads on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of PomTom after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
